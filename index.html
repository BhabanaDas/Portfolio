The Engineering Journey

Silicon & Hardware

I spend a lot of my time in Cadence Virtuoso and LTspice, simulating the tiny switches that run the world. My focus has been on dynamic latched comparators and CMOS logic—balancing speed with power efficiency.

"Precision in the smallest details leads to stability in the largest systems."

Human Signals (ECG/PPG)

One of my favorite projects involves health tech. I analyze biological signals like ECG and PPG to understand how we can use electronics to monitor and improve human health in real-time.

"Turning heartbeats into actionable data."

Data & Market Analysis

I don't just build hardware; I like to understand the market for it. My analysis of the laptop industry and ongoing fraud detection projects show my curiosity for how data protects and moves our economy.

"Using ML to find the truth hidden in transaction patterns."
